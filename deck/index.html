What they do today
Hold NVDA idle, or LP by hand: pick the pool, the fee tier and the price range, then read a fee APR with no loss column.
The wedge
Kept APR, measured per token on 3,059,881 replayed swaps, on a share Morpho can take as collateral. 3 open, 9 reduced, 5 closed.
Why now
22 October: Robinhood made Stock Tokens Morpho collateral, "any app can tap into these markets". Morpho Blue holds $609.5M of the chain's $1.14B.
Alternatives and gaps
HedgeHood: weekly covered calls on NVDA. TickerSpring, AutoRange: USDG in. Morpho: lenders earn, collateral does not. None shows kept APR.
Why onchain, why Arbitrum
Robinhood Chain is an Arbitrum Orbit chain. The Stock Token, its Chainlink feed and the USDG pool share one chain, so valuation and exit need no bridge. Agents read it over MCP.
