# Uptick interviews

15 minute script for Stock Token holders and Robinhood Chain LPs. The goal is a verbatim record of the job, the pain, the current alternative and one sized, non-binding intent. It is not a pitch.

## Jobs to test

These are hypotheses. A row in the log is evidence only after a real call with a date and a URL.

| Segment | Functional job | Emotional job | Social job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stock Token holder | Keep NVDA or GOOGL exposure while the position earns something | Stop feeling behind for leaving the tokens idle | Keep up with peers who already LP or earn on their stock tokens |
| Robinhood Chain LP | Earn fees without watching ranges all day | Avoid the feeling of being picked off by fast traders | Be treated as a serious LP, not a yield tourist |

## Before the call

- One interviewer. Notes or recording with consent.
- Do not describe Uptick until the closing ask. If asked early, answer in one sentence: "Vaults that LP Stock Tokens against USDG on Robinhood Chain and publish kept APR after markout." Then return to their last answer.
- Ask about what happened, not what they would hypothetically do, until 0:12.
- Record the exact words for pain and desired outcome. Do not upgrade "maybe" into "yes".
- Log the row only after the call. No interview, no row.

## The script

### 0:00 to 0:02, frame

"Thanks for 15 minutes. I am researching how people hold and earn on Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain. I want to hear what you actually do, not what you think I want to hear. There are no right answers. Can I take notes?"

### 0:02 to 0:05, last real behavior

"Walk me through the last time you bought or held NVDA, GOOGL, or another Stock Token on Robinhood Chain. What did you do with it after the buy?"

Probes:
- "What else did you consider at that moment?"
- "What did you actually do with the tokens while you held them?"
- "Was there a point where you moved them, or left them alone? Tell me about that."

### 0:05 to 0:08, pain in their words

"Tell me about the last time holding or LPing those tokens cost you money, time, or sleep."

Probes:
- "What happened, in order?"
- "What did you do about it?"
- "What was the most annoying part?"
- "What did that cost you, in fees, time, or attention?"
- "What have you tried since?"

Do not supply a pain. If they say "nothing", ask what they did with the position instead and move on.

### 0:08 to 0:10, the three jobs

Functional:
- "What are you trying to get done with that position?"
- "What would done look like on a normal week?"

Emotional:
- "What worries you when the position just sits there, or when a range is running?"
- "What would let you stop checking it?"

Social:
- "Who do you compare notes with on this?"
- "What do they expect you to do with idle stock tokens?"
- "What would you not want them to see?"

### 0:10 to 0:12, triggers and alternatives

Triggers:
- "What made you start LPing, or start looking for yield?"
- "What made you stop, or move size?"
- "What would make you move size in the next week?"

Alternatives, read the list and let them pick:
- "What do you use today: nothing, Robinhood Earn, manual LP, another vault, a friend, or something else?"
- "Why that one?"
- "What do you pay for it, in fees, time, or attention?"
- "What would make you switch?"

### 0:12 to 0:14, desired outcome in their words

- "If this worked exactly as you wanted, what would you check on a Monday?"
- "What number would tell you it is working?"
- "What would make you tell a friend about it?"
- "What would make you pull out?"

### 0:14 to 0:15, the closing ask

"I am asking for a one page non-binding intent, not money. It records the token and the size you would honestly consider if the conditions are met, plus the conditions themselves. It commits nothing. Would you sign one?"

Then:
- "What size is honest today, not the size you would like to do later?"
- "What condition matters most: a kept APR floor, an audit, a deposit cap, or exit time?"
- "What kept APR would you need to see before you would deposit?"
- "Can I follow up when that condition is met?"
- "Who else should I talk to?"

Close with the LOI template in `LOI-TEMPLATES.md`. Send it the same day. A verbal maybe is not an intent.
